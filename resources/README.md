# Resources

Original icons, presets, translations and templates (spec §5.5). Nothing ships here yet; the first
assets are the Stage 1 tool and panel icons in `resources/icons/` (S1-7.8, ADR-0028).

Rules (spec §2.3, §5.3):

- Original artwork only. Do not copy another product's icons, logos, screenshots or bundled brushes.
- Every third-party font, brush, palette, profile or fixture needs an entry in
  `docs/dependencies/manifest.yaml` with its source, license and redistribution terms before it is
  committed.
